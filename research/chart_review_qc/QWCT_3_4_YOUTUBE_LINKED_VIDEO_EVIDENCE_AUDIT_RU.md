## IMPORTANT new practical evidence: dynamic price-reanchoring is real

The official [May 20 YouTube recap](https://gerchik.co/ru/analytics/tehnicheskiy-analiz/razbor-sdelok-s-aleksandrom-gerchikom-20-05-2026) explicitly recounts:
- **Case 2:** Five repeated attempts at a short zone did not follow through; the **sixth short entry** still relied on the wrong area. Its [official screenshot](https://gerchik.co/file/4927) visibly shows **BTCUSDT** daily/M5, **NOT US equities**. The recap attributes instruction to **adapt the level around a false-break point and trade THAT new line**, rather than keep recycling old attempts.
- **Case 13 (IMPORTANT: genuine US-listed stock):** the official attached screenshot at [gerchik.co/file/4937](https://gerchik.co/file/4937) visibly shows **MMS (MAXIMUS Inc., NYSE)**, in several chart views. The written recap says the wrong level was drawn **too high** despite repeated penetrations; move the reference lower to **where price actually stopped**. This materially changes stop geometry. Screenshot DOES NOT reveal a fully reproducible before/after *spoken* demonstration or independently validated quote precision of the suggested new price. **This observation is directly relevant to QWCT US equities, unlike the BTC/Forex cases**, but still no automatic price selection rule.
- **Case 3/9/14:** Heavy, repeatedly chopped levels or exhaustive approach movement are contexts where a line may exist yet be **not usable for immediate entry**. Level existence ≠ executable setup.

The official [July 1 YouTube recap](https://gerchik.co/ru/analytics/tehnicheskiy-analiz/obzor-rynkov-s-aleksandrom-gerchikom-ot-01-07-2026) provides an even more distinctive structure:
- **USD/JPY:** when a level is being sawed through, **reassessment/redrawing after observing the day close**, not persistent static line regardless of new facts.
- **USD/CAD:** the old repeatedly penetrated level is **no longer the same defended reference**. A **new** defended level at **141.78** is supported by exact repeats plus a two-bar false-break pattern. This is NOT an ATR cluster median. Different market, and quote must not be imported literally to equities.
- **GBP/NZD:** quoted exact **2.1578** level.
- **S&P500:** official recap describes opens/closes repeatedly near a line as qualitative evidence the line was correctly placed; **does NOT waive the exact BPU1 touch for the specific bounce model**.

### Consequences for QWCT

1. **New distinction:** `LEVEL_EXISTS` vs `LEVEL_CURRENTLY_DEFENDED` vs `SETUP_APPROVED`. Current software `kind='mirror'` from D1 close-breach+retest alone proves none of the latter two.
2. **Re-anchoring is not selecting arbitrary FIRST/LAST/MEDIAN:** author’s associated **specific event** (false break / actual defended turning point) may supply a **NEW version** of a price AFTER THAT event is observed. A level formerly protected at an old price can become chopped and cease to be tradeable. Future M5 outcomes must never select a past as-of D1 line.
3. **Causal implementation discipline (our engineering inference, NOT an explicit Gerchik API spec):** whenever a candidate price is revised, create a new `(source_bar_time, confirmed_at, effective_from, exact_price, reason)`. Freeze every historical day's as-of level. Re-evaluate candidate validity only from information available at that time. Do not modify existing production, because explicit re-anchor decision function remains unverified.
4. **Specific role of false-break reference:** documented actual live-trade recap calls out a false-break point as possible anchor. **Proposed investigation**: on independently author-annotated cases, determine whether reference comes from the false-break bar's high/low, last defended bar, its close, or another price. **NOT safe to assume it is universally the retest candle low/high.**
5. **Independent nuance from previously recorded source work:** the question “must our software pick **one** historical BSU before BPU1?” remains unresolved. These broadcasts **reinforce contextual source choice**, but do not prove that a frozen set of FIRST/LAST candidates is sufficient or correct.
